# Watch My Plot insurance broker brief

Prepared: 26 October 2026. This is separate from the legal review. It is a request for written insurance advice, not an assertion that a policy is legally compulsory.

## Business description

Watch My Plot is an online marketplace connecting adult garden owners with adult self-employed or independent gardeners. Owners and gardeners agree the work, access arrangements and price. The owner pays through Stripe; the gardener pays a 15% platform fee plus Stripe charges. The platform does not employ gardeners, direct their work or currently promise insurance or a reimbursement guarantee.

The initial territories are the United Kingdom, Australia and New Zealand. Gardeners may enter private gardens, allotments or similar plots while owners are absent and may handle gates, keys and temporary access arrangements. Work can involve plants, crops, lawns, greenhouses, ponds, tools, garden structures, irrigation and domestic property near the work area.

## Written advice requested for the platform

Please state whether each cover is available, recommended or required, its territorial scope, key exclusions, excess and indicative premium:

- Public liability for Watch My Plot's own activities, including events, demonstrations, visits or staff activity rather than the gardeners' work.
- Technology errors and omissions or professional indemnity for the marketplace, booking information, communications and platform failures.
- Cyber and privacy cover for data breach, incident response, regulatory investigation, ransomware, payment fraud and business interruption.
- Marketplace or contingent liability where a claim alleges Watch My Plot is responsible for a gardener's act, selection, representations or inadequate controls.
- Crime, fidelity and social-engineering cover for theft allegations, fraudulent users, payment diversion or misuse of keys/access information.
- Directors' and officers' cover if the operator is a limited company.
- Legal-expenses cover for consumer, contract, employment-status and regulatory disputes.
- Employers' liability if anyone later becomes an employee, worker or apprentice, including the point at which cover must start.
- Any cover needed for platform-organised training, college recruitment events or work-experience arrangements.

Please confirm whether policy wording would be affected by the platform recommending insurance without verifying it, allowing uninsured gardeners, operating in three countries, using Stripe Connect direct charges, or describing gardeners as independent.

## Minimum gardener cover to ask brokers about

Please advise on a suitable individual gardener policy and whether Watch My Plot should later make proof compulsory. The policy should be checked expressly for:

- accidental injury and property damage at customers' homes, gardens and allotments;
- damage to plants, crops, trees, lawns, soil, irrigation, greenhouses, glass, fences, sheds, ponds and nearby buildings;
- negligent overwatering, underwatering, pruning, spraying, chemical use, frost/heat protection or failure to follow instructions;
- care, custody and control exclusions that could remove cover for the very plot or property being worked on;
- keys, replacement locks, alarm/access devices and allegations of theft or unauthorised access;
- unattended access while an owner is away;
- hand tools, powered tools, mowers, ladders, work at height, pesticides, herbicides and waste removal;
- injury or damage caused by helpers, subcontractors or temporary workers;
- business use of a vehicle and carriage of tools, waste or chemicals;
- legal defence costs and allegations later shown to be unfounded;
- cover in the UK, Australia and New Zealand where relevant, rather than UK-only cover;
- work for consumers through an online marketplace and any minimum qualifications or risk controls.

## Claims scenarios for the broker to answer

1. A gardener uses the wrong treatment and destroys a crop or established plants.
2. Watering instructions are missed while the owner is away and plants die.
3. A greenhouse pane, irrigation system, fence or neighbouring property is damaged.
4. A key is lost, an alarm code is exposed or locks must be replaced.
5. The owner alleges that property was stolen during unattended access.
6. A gardener or third party is injured using a ladder, mower or chemical.
7. The platform is sued alongside the gardener for negligent selection, misleading statements or failure to respond to a warning.
8. Personal access information or private messages are exposed in a cyber incident.

For every scenario, please identify which proposed policy responds, who is insured, whether the owner must claim first on home/allotment insurance, whether subrogation could be pursued, and every material exclusion.

## Current public position

Until written cover is purchased and its wording verified, Watch My Plot will continue to state that it provides no insurance or booking guarantee. It will recommend gardener insurance without saying gardeners are insured or verified. Terms will preserve liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded and will not treat a disclaimer as a substitute for risk controls or insurance.
