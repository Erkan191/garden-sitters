# Watch My Plot legal review pack

Prepared: 26 October 2026. This is a factual hand-off for a UK solicitor, not legal advice.

## Business and launch position

- Watch My Plot is an online marketplace for adult garden owners and adult gardeners in the UK, Australia and New Zealand.
- Registration is free. The owner pays the displayed booking price. The gardener pays Watch My Plot 15% of the job price plus Stripe processing charges.
- Payments use Stripe Connect direct charges on the gardener's connected Stripe account. Watch My Plot takes an application fee.
- Watch My Plot is not the gardener's employer and does not direct how gardening work is performed. The legal classification must nevertheless be assessed from the real relationship, not this label.
- Watch My Plot supplies no platform insurance or reimbursement guarantee. Gardeners are recommended, but not initially required, to hold suitable insurance.
- Public listings show broad locations only. Exact addresses and access arrangements are exchanged privately after agreement. Users are told not to put alarm codes or exact hidden-key locations on the platform.
- The service is strictly 18+.

## Current booking and cancellation design

- Immediately before Stripe Checkout, the owner sees the total, no-insurance position and cancellation summary and accepts the versioned terms and policy.
- The owner also expressly requests early performance if work is due to start within a statutory cancellation period and is warned about proportionate payment and completion of the service.
- Consent timestamps and policy versions are stored in the booking and copied to Stripe metadata.
- A durable email confirmation is sent to the owner and gardener after successful payment.
- If either party cancels before any work begins, the owner receives a full refund. The owner-facing non-refundable amount is £0, A$0 or NZ$0 and Watch My Plot returns its fee.
- If the gardener fails to attend and no service is supplied, the owner receives a full refund.
- Once work starts, mandatory consumer rights apply. Quality, plant/crop loss, damage and partially completed-work claims are assessed from the agreement, messages, photographs and other evidence. Watch My Plot does not promise compensation.
- Stripe refund, failed-payment, dispute and chargeback events are recorded automatically against the booking. Issuing a refund is currently a support/admin action rather than an automatic transfer of money.

## Questions requiring legal review

1. Confirm the legal operator's name, business form, service address, company number and VAT number (if applicable), and the exact disclosures required on every relevant page and confirmation.
2. Confirm whether each gardener must declare whether they act as a trader or private individual, how this must be displayed before booking, and which consumer obligations follow.
3. Confirm the Consumer Contracts Regulations analysis for: the platform contract; the owner-gardener service contract; bookings beginning within 14 days; services fully performed inside 14 days; and owner-to-private-individual bookings.
4. Confirm that the single pre-payment acceptance and express early-performance request are sufficiently prominent and separate, or specify the exact checkbox wording required.
5. Review the full-refund-before-start launch rule, post-start proportional-payment wording, complaint process and model cancellation wording.
6. Review the marketplace allocation of responsibility, user indemnity, suspension and liability clauses for fairness and enforceability. No clause attempts to exclude fraud, death or personal injury caused by negligence, or mandatory consumer rights.
7. Advise on reasonable retention periods for booking messages, dispute evidence, safety reports and tax/platform reporting records.
8. Confirm privacy-controller identity, international-transfer documentation, processor contracts, data-subject process, breach process and whether a UK representative or overseas privacy notices are required.
9. Review the Australian and New Zealand offering before active promotion, including mandatory consumer guarantees, marketplace disclosures and privacy jurisdiction.
10. Confirm whether garden visits, key handling, pesticides, powered tools, waste carriage or particular gardening activities create licensing, health-and-safety or other duties for the platform or gardeners.

## Pre-solicitor actions for the operator

- Supply the operator's verified legal name and geographic service address. Do not invent or conceal these details in the published terms or privacy notice.
- Complete the ICO data-protection fee self-assessment and pay/register if required.
- Determine the platform operator's business form for HMRC digital-platform reporting. The UK registration and reporting position differs for a sole trader operator and a company or partnership.
- Document a complaints and refund runbook: identity check, booking evidence, no-work confirmation, refund amount, application-fee return, Stripe action, user notification and booking audit record.
- Maintain a versioned archive of every terms and cancellation policy used for a paid booking.
- Record whether gardeners are acting as traders and show the result to owners before payment once the solicitor confirms the wording.
- Do not market the platform or gardeners as checked, insured, guaranteed, vetted or DBS-checked unless the relevant process is actually operating and evidenced.

## Cat in a Flat comparison (pre-Rover)

The archived 12 December 2025 Cat in a Flat terms are useful as market evidence, not a template to copy.

- Cat in a Flat described itself as an introduction/matching service and said it was not party to the owner-sitter agreement or the sitter's employer.
- It named the operating company and registered office.
- It used Stripe and published fixed cancellation tiers: more than 16 days, no charge; 15–8 days, a fixed £1.50 charge; seven days or fewer, 30% with a £9.50 minimum; no refund during the booking.
- A sitter cancellation refund was stated to be discretionary.
- It offered a separate limited Cat in a Flat Guarantee, including stated vet and lost-key limits and conditions. It did not simply rely on a no-liability clause.
- Its liability limits, releases and indemnities should not be copied without a fairness and enforceability review.
- Its archived privacy notice was broad and dated and is not an adequate modern template.

Current Cat in a Flat bookings refer to the Rover Guarantee. Rover expressly describes that as a reimbursement guarantee rather than insurance. Rover's guarantee also excludes crops, timber and natural or cultivated land features, making it a poor fit for Watch My Plot's central garden and crop risks.
